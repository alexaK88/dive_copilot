Most voice agents replace typing because typing is inconvenient. Dive Copilot replaces typing where typing is physically impossible.

Dive Copilot is a hands-free AI mission assistant that lets scientific and professional divers record observations, access mission information, and communicate structured information to the surface without interrupting underwater work.


Users:
    - marine researchers
    - environmental survey teams
    - underwater inspection teams
    - aquaculture operations
    - scientific dive teams
    - commercial diving operations
